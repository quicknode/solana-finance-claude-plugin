One position, built in loops

A thousand USDC becomes nearly two thousand dollars of NVIDIA. The stock is the collateral; the dollars are the debt.

Buy NVDAx and deposit it
Tokenized NVIDIA stock goes into the lending market as collateral
Borrow USDC against it
Up to the market's loan-to-value: 50% of what the collateral is worth
Buy more NVDAx, deposit again, repeat
Each round the position grows — and the next borrow shrinks
